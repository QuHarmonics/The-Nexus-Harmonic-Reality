## What this domain covers

Materials research addresses how local constraints and transformations relate to larger-scale structure: defects, interfaces, phase change, transport, and retained state. In NEXUS terms, this is an application space where the mapping from an abstract operation to a material observable must be explicit.

**Relationship to the foundation:** the [NEXUS Domain](../The%20Nexus%20Domain/) defines the native machine. Research here explores particular constructions, applications, or comparators without changing that domain by assumption.

## How to read the work

1. Specify the material, conditions, and scale.
2. Define the measurement or simulation readout.
3. Keep structural arguments separate from validated property predictions.
4. Record uncertainty, boundary effects, and the source of measurements.

> **Boundary:** A modeled structure is not a measured material property without a reproducible comparison.

**Research ledger:** **STANDS** = proved identity or external theorem; **RUNS** = executed readout with stated scope; **OPEN** = missing derivation/link; **DEAD END** = observed landing owned elsewhere; **PULL** = external or physical interpretation. These words are not interchangeable.

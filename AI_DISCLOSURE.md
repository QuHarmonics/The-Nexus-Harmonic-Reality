# AI-assisted research disclosure

NEXUS is a human-directed research program developed through iterative collaboration with AI systems. Dean A. Kulik directs the conceptual investigation, identifies constraints and gaps, develops architectural relationships, and uses AI tools in derivation, drafting, coding, analysis and documentation. Individual artifacts may contain substantial AI-generated content.

Preserve source conversations, versions, corrections, authorship information and actual execution records where available. Distinguish a human proposal, an AI suggestion, a mathematical derivation, and an executed result. A model response is not an execution certificate.

The nature and extent of human authorship vary by artifact. Attribution to the research program is not a blanket assertion of copyright in every AI-generated expression. Rights and publication permissions are assessed at the work level.

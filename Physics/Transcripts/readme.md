## Purpose

The reasoning and audit record behind physical hypotheses, comparisons, and revisions. This collection belongs to the **Physics** research area and should remain identifiable as that area develops.

## Audit and provenance

Transcripts preserve how a result was developed: predictions, arguments, tool calls, failed routes, corrections, and revisions. They are supporting history, **not** the preferred front door for readers and **not** substitutes for executable tests.

Before adding raw conversations, review them for private identifiers, credentials, third-party confidential material, and accidental disclosures. Keep original ordering and timestamps when practical; do not edit out a failed readout and then claim an uninterrupted landing.

## Reading rule

**Use transcripts to inspect provenance and revision history.** External physics is a comparator, not a tuning knob for the core; physical interpretation remains an adapter unless a bridge is explicitly derived.

**Research ledger:** **STANDS** = proved identity or external theorem; **RUNS** = executed readout with stated scope; **OPEN** = missing derivation/link; **DEAD END** = observed landing owned elsewhere; **PULL** = external or physical interpretation. These words are not interchangeable.

## In this research area

- Name the exact NEXUS observable being compared.
- State units, calibration, parameter choices, and the dataset or theorem used.
- Distinguish mathematical analogy from a physical derivation or prediction.
- Keep corrections and independent measurements alongside the interpretation.

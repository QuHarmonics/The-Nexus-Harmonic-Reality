   Another color-changing chemical oscillator. You get an eerie amber-blue-clear repeating pattern without adding more stuff — just energy stored and released in cycles.

3. **Autocatalysis loops**  
   Where the product of a reaction helps speed up or trigger more of the same reaction. If set up right, these can create feedback loops — think of a fire feeding itself, but chemically.

4. **Phase-locked oscillators in biochemistry**  
   Like circadian rhythms or glycolysis in cells — reactions phase-locked over time without needing a constant outside nudge. That’s how your body knows it’s time to sleep or wake up, for example.

For a 50W car speaker, the gauge of the speaker wire does matter, but not drastically within reasonable limits. Here’s a breakdown:

### **Wire Gauge Recommendations for 50W Speakers:**
- **18 AWG (American Wire Gauge)**: Sufficient for most car audio applications. It can handle up to about 50 feet with minimal resistance.
- **16 AWG**: Provides better conductivity and lower resistance, ideal if your speaker wires are longer than 20 feet.
- **14 AWG**: Overkill for 50W speakers unless you’re running long distances (above 50 feet), but it can still be used for added durability.

### **Why It Matters:**
- **Resistance:** Thicker wires (lower gauge number) have less resistance, meaning less signal loss and better sound quality.
- **Power Efficiency:** If the wire is too thin, it may cause a voltage drop, affecting speaker performance.
- **Installation & Flexibility:** Thinner wires (18-16 AWG) are easier to route in tight spaces, making them more practical for car audio.

### **Bottom Line:**
For **most car audio setups with 50W speakers**, **18 AWG** is fine, **16 AWG is ideal**, and **14 AWG is only necessary for long runs or future-proofing**.

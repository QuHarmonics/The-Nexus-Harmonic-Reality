Great question — both **3M 90** and **3M 94** are adhesive products, but they serve **very different purposes**, especially when working with foam, upholstery, and leather like in your E36 seat restoration.

Here’s a clear breakdown:

---

## 🆚 3M Super 90 vs 3M Primer 94

### 🟢 **3M Super 90 Spray Adhesive**
- **Purpose**: High-strength contact adhesive
- **Type**: Spray-based
- **Use Case**: Bonding foam, fabric, leather, plastic, wood, etc.
- **Dry Time**: Fast-tack (~1 minute), repositionable briefly
- **Strength**: Very strong initial grab; semi-permanent
- **Best For**: Upholstery, foam-to-foam or foam-to-fabric, headliners, seat bolsters

> ✅ *What you want for adding foam layers under leather.*  
> Super 90 bonds fast and firm, especially good when you need leather to stick to dense foam contours.

---

### 🟡 **3M Primer 94**
- **Purpose**: Surface adhesion promoter
- **Type**: Liquid primer (not an adhesive)
- **Use Case**: Prepping low-energy surfaces like plastics (e.g., polypropylene) before applying **tapes or vinyl wraps**
- **Dry Time**: ~5–10 min before top application
- **Strength**: Enhances tape grip; not a glue
- **Best For**: Vinyl wrap adhesion, plastic trim, automotive moldings

> ❌ *Not appropriate for bonding foam or leather.*  
> Primer 94 is not an adhesive — it prepares the surface for **tape**, not for upholstery work.

---

## 🧠 TL;DR:

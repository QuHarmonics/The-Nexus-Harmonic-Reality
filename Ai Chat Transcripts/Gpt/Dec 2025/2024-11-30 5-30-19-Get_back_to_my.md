To switch back to your standard account, you can follow these general steps depending on the platform or service you're using:

### For Online Platforms or Accounts:
1. **Log Out of the Current Account:**
   - Locate the "Log Out" or "Sign Out" option, usually in the profile or settings menu of the service.

2. **Log In with Your Standard Account:**
   - Go to the login page and enter your credentials for the standard account.

3. **Check Remembered Accounts:**
   - If the platform allows account switching (e.g., Google, Microsoft), select your standard account from the list of remembered accounts.

### For Local Computer Accounts:
- **Windows:**
  1. Press `Ctrl + Alt + Delete` and select "Switch User."
  2. Choose your standard account from the list of users.
  3. Enter your password to log in.

- **MacOS:**
  1. Click on the Apple menu and select "Log Out."
  2. Log in with your standard user account from the login screen.

### For Social Media Apps (e.g., Instagram, Facebook):
1. Go to the account menu in the app.
2. If you've linked multiple accounts, tap on your standard account to switch back.
3. If not linked, log out and log in again using your standard account credentials.

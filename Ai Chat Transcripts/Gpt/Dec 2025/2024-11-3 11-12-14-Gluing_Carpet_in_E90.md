can i glue German Velour Carpet Backless (vinyl back) carpet over my existing formed carpet in my e90?

## ASSISTANT
It’s generally not advisable to glue a new layer of carpet, even with a vinyl back, directly over existing formed carpet, especially in a car like your BMW E90. Here’s why:

1. **Adhesion Issues**: Gluing over a formed carpet (especially if it's plush) can lead to poor adhesion because of the contours and padding beneath the original carpet. The new carpet may not sit properly and could start to peel up over time, particularly in high-traffic areas.

2. **Aesthetic and Fit**: The original carpet is molded to fit the car’s floor pan, while adding a layer on top could make the interior look uneven and may not follow the car’s contours as intended.

3. **Potential for Odors**: Adhesive between two carpet layers can trap moisture and dirt, potentially leading to unwanted odors and even mold if not carefully managed.

4. **Alternative Solutions**:
   - **Full Removal and Replacement**: If you're set on having the German Velour Carpet, the best approach would be to remove the existing carpet entirely and replace it with the new one. This will ensure a clean, custom fit without the risks of layering.
   - **Custom-Fit Floor Mats**: For added texture and luxury, custom-fit velour floor mats over your existing carpet might provide the look and feel you’re after without needing to glue anything down.

If you’re interested in a DIY solution that could add to the aesthetics without compromising fit, custom mats are a popular choice among E90 enthusiasts!

1. **Authoritarian Leadership** – Centralized control under a single leader or ruling party, with limited or no checks on power.  
2. **Ultranationalism** – A belief in the supremacy of the nation or a particular group, often tied to exclusionary or xenophobic ideologies.  
3. **Suppression of Opposition** – Censorship, propaganda, intimidation, and violence used to silence political rivals, independent media, and dissenting voices.  
4. **Militarism** – Glorification of military strength and the use of force as a tool for national renewal or expansion.  
5. **Corporatism / Controlled Economy** – The state exerts heavy influence or direct control over economic activity, sometimes in partnership with large corporations, while rejecting both free-market liberalism and socialist redistribution.  
6. **Cult of Tradition and Myth** – Reliance on symbolic imagery, historical myths, or appeals to a glorious past to justify present actions.  
7. **Mass Mobilization** – Encouraging citizens to participate in rallies, marches, and rituals that emphasize loyalty to the leader and state.  

The term originated in Italy under Benito Mussolini in the early 20th century, but it has since been applied to various movements and regimes that share these core traits.

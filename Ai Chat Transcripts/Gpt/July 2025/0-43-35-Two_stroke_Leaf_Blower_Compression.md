## USER
how much compresion shold a small 2stroke leaf blower  have

## ASSISTANT
A small two-stroke leaf blower typically should have compression in the range of **90 to 110 psi (pounds per square inch)**. Good compression ensures that the engine can start easily and run efficiently. If the compression is significantly lower than this range (below 80 psi), it may indicate worn piston rings, a damaged cylinder, or a faulty crankshaft seal, which could result in poor engine performance or difficulty starting.

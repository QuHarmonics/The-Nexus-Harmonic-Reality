## ASSISTANT
Since your **2010 BMW E90 335i** has the **Base Stereo System** with no external amplifier, the **head unit does not have dedicated line-level (low-voltage, preamp) outputs**. Instead, it **only provides high-level (speaker-level) outputs**, which directly power the speakers.  

### **Technical Details of the Base Stereo System Head Unit (RAD2 or CIC iDrive)**
- **No RCA or dedicated line-level outputs**  
- **High-level speaker outputs** (designed to drive speakers directly)  
- **Four speaker channels (front left, front right, rear left, rear right)**  
- **Impedance**: The factory speakers are typically **2-ohm**, unlike standard aftermarket 4-ohm speakers  
- **AUX input in center console** (passive, no preamp involved)  

### **How to Add an Amplifier or Aftermarket Audio System?**  
Since there are no line-level outputs, you have a few options to integrate an amplifier or aftermarket audio upgrades:  

1. **Use a Line Output Converter (LOC)**  
   - Converts high-level speaker outputs to low-level RCA preamp signals for an amplifier.  
   - Example: **AudioControl LC2i**, PAC SNI-35, or similar.  
   - This method allows you to add an aftermarket amplifier while retaining the stock head unit.  

2. **Use an Amplifier with High-Level (Speaker-Level) Inputs**  
   - Many modern amplifiers accept high-level inputs, removing the need for an LOC.  
   - Example: JL Audio, Alpine, and Rockford Fosgate amplifiers with speaker-level input support.  

3. **Upgrade to an Aftermarket Head Unit** (More Involved)  
   - Requires a **custom wiring harness** (since the factory head unit integrates multiple vehicle functions).  
   - Can provide **true RCA preamp outputs** for cleaner audio signal transmission.  

### **Conclusion**  
Your factory head unit does not have **line-level outputs**, so adding an amplifier requires either a **Line Output Converter (LOC)** or an amplifier with **high-level inputs**. If your goal is **improved audio quality**, using an LOC with a DSP (Digital Sound Processor) is highly recommended to compensate for BMW’s factory equalization settings.

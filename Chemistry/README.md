## What this domain covers

This area is for NEXUS applications to chemistry, including molecular structure, reaction pathways, symmetry, constraints, and the emergence of stable configurations. The focus is to connect an exact domain construction to a named chemical system through a testable mapping.

**Relationship to the foundation:** the [NEXUS Domain](../The%20Nexus%20Domain/) defines the native machine. Research here explores particular constructions, applications, or comparators without changing that domain by assumption.

## How to read the work

1. Identify the chemical species, state, and reaction conditions.
2. Document energies, units, methods, and boundary conditions.
3. Distinguish schematic geometry from a quantitative prediction.
4. Retain measurements or references needed to reproduce comparisons.

> **Boundary:** A binary transformation is not by itself a chemical reaction model; the physical bridge must be specified.

**Research ledger:** **STANDS** = proved identity or external theorem; **RUNS** = executed readout with stated scope; **OPEN** = missing derivation/link; **DEAD END** = observed landing owned elsewhere; **PULL** = external or physical interpretation. These words are not interchangeable.

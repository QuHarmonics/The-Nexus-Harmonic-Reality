# QuHarmonics Research-First Licensing Policy

Research by Dean A. Kulik · QuHarmonics Research Group · [ORCID 0009-0003-3128-8828](https://orcid.org/0009-0003-3128-8828)

## Research first

NEXUS is intended to benefit independent researchers, students, educators, hobbyists and noncommercial builders first. The objective is to make research and reproducible implementations accessible without a fee for permitted noncommercial use, while retaining the ability to negotiate commercial reuse of material controlled by its rights holders.

Commercial activity is not inherently unwelcome. Commercial licensing can support the continuation of research. No royalty rate, commercial contract or mandatory contribution amount is established by this policy.

## Commercial permission

For a proposed commercial use that requires permission beyond an applicable license, contact the rights holder through [QuHarmonics](https://quharmonics.com/). Identify the material, revision, proposed use and distribution model. Separate authorization may be granted on a case-by-case basis. Do not assume that a request constitutes permission.

## Existing rights and limits

Existing licenses and permissions remain in force, including irrevocable grants as provided by their terms. This policy does not withdraw, narrow or supersede an earlier license. Material without an identified grant remains subject to applicable law and any permissions otherwise available; do not infer a license merely from public availability. Nothing here restricts statutory exceptions, public-domain material, independently developed implementations, facts, mathematical ideas, principles, discoveries or methods that are not protected by copyright. No ownership of mathematics itself is asserted.

The historical collection contains multiple versions, AI-assisted material and possible third-party content. New license notices must identify the exact works and rights controlled by the licensor. No historical files are relicensed by this policy.

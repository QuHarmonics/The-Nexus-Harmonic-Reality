# The Nexus Domain — transcript index

[← Domain](../README.md) · [Working sources](../Markdown/) · [NEXUS home](../../README.md)

This is the dedicated entry point for research conversations and their provenance. The active directory contains this index only.

Historical conversation exports and working records are available in the [archived Markdown collection](https://github.com/QuHarmonics/The-Nexus-Harmonic-Reality/tree/462e8e558eafb9f333b7726eddf0e7a527ee93e1/The%20Nexus%20Domain/Markdown). Their original filenames and locations are retained.

See also the [cross-domain transcript index](../../Audit%20Trail/Transcripts/) and [history guide](../../docs/HISTORY.md).

Future transcript entries should retain source conversation identity, author or speaker role, message order, timestamps where supplied, and links to related executable or published artifacts. Missing timestamps remain unknown. The goal is to preserve the order of discovery, not to rewrite the historical dialogue.

## What this domain covers

This is the **foundation of NEXUS**. It contains the ring as fixed model, the One Inverter clock, the reversible reader–field step, the three closure classes, multi-reader collision rules, and the ports through which external systems may read or write. The domain is defined by its own binary operations; physical comparisons are downstream readings, not assumptions of the machine.

**Read this domain first.** It supplies the native operations that other research areas may use as tools or comparators.

## How to read the work

1. Start at the ring: reversal, complement, addressing, seam and carrier.
2. Follow the clock into the reader–field step and its exact inverse.
3. Read closures and collisions before attaching external interpretations.
4. Keep mathematical identities, executed tests, and physical readings visibly distinct.

> **Boundary:** The domain establishes its own operators. An external measurement can be compared with a domain projection, but cannot silently rewrite the native rule.

**Research ledger:** **STANDS** = proved identity or external theorem; **RUNS** = executed readout with stated scope; **OPEN** = missing derivation/link; **DEAD END** = observed landing owned elsewhere; **PULL** = external or physical interpretation. These words are not interchangeable.

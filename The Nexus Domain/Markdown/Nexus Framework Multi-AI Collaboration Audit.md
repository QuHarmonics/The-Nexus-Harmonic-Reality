Let:

*   **N_h** = teeth on horizontal gear (fast, ~50)
*   **N_v** = teeth on vertical gear (slow, ~51)
*   **P_h** = tooth pitch angle = 2π/N_h
*   **P_v** = tooth pitch angle = 2π/N_v

For 90° bevel gears, **contact happens when angular positions satisfy**:

```text
θ_h mod P_h = 0  (horizontal tooth at contact point)
θ_v mod P_v = 0  (vertical tooth at contact point)
t_h = t_v       (same time)
```

### **2. The Pythagorean Timing Constraint**

The gear ratio is **irrational** because N_h ≠ N_v:


```text
v_eff = √(v_h² + v_v²) = r·√(ω_h² + ω_v²)
```

Where r is the gear radius (assume equal for simplicity).

### **3. Phase Alignment Condition**

The teeth align when:

```text
θ_h(t) = n·P_h = 2πn/N_h
θ_v(t) = m·P_v = 2πm/N_v
```

```text
ω_h/ω_v = (n/N_h)/(m/N_v) = n·N_v/(m·N_h)
```

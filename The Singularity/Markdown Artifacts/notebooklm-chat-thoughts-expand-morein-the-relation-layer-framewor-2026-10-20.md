### 2\. Disambiguating "Zero": Three Distinct Algebraic Causes

The deficiency module D\=A/W separates zero readings into three mathematically distinct conditions\[11\]\[12\]:

1.  **Genuine Absence** (mλ​(A)\=0): The irreducible representation type λ does not exist in the ambient module A at all\[12\]\[13\]. On the 12-vertex witness arena, m(A)\=(15,4,14,4,24,36), meaning every rational irreducible occurs with positive multiplicity and genuine absence never occurs\[5\].
2.  **Vocabulary Deficiency** (mλ​(W)<mλ​(A)): The declared relation vocabulary W fails to span the available multiplicity in channel λ\[12\]\[13\].
3.  **Reader-Induced Flatness / Blindness** (mλ​(D)\>0, but dimλH\=0): The obstruction exists in full within D, but the chosen reader H has no fixed-vector readout in that channel (bin 0)\[12\]\[13\].

_"The most symmetric reader is the blindest"_: The full automorphism reader G\=Aut(X) projects strictly onto the trivial irreducible sector 1+, masking higher-order obstruction types (such as the 3− sign-twisted obstruction) that remain active in non-trivial channels\[15\].

### 3\. The Physical Operator Gap: The 22-Parameter Weight Family

When moving upstream from static cell counts to system dynamics, the token arena does not uniquely select a single linear evolution operator\[19\]\[20\].

-   **The 22 Edge Orbits**: Enforcing G\-equivariance, radius-one locality, self-adjointness, and total conservation (T1\=0) on the witness arena leaves a **22-parameter weight family**, matching the 22 distinct G\-edge orbits of the arena\[19\].
-   **The Laplacian as an Imported Homogeneity Assumption**: The standard graph Laplacian L\=D−A is merely the single isolated point where all 22 edge-orbit weights are set equal\[19\]. Selecting L imports an un-derived assumption of edge-transitivity on an arena that is not edge-transitive\[20\]\[21\].
-   **Exclusion via Invariant Potentials**: Requiring transformation prices to be generated by an equivariant potential (p\=dϕ) restricts the parameter space from 22 parameters down to 7 free parameters (spanning an 18-dimensional weight set ∣dϕ∣)\[19\].
-   **Local Exclusion Mechanism**: Exactly 1 of the 22 edge orbits lies entirely inside a single G\-vertex orbit\[23\]\[26\]. For any G\-invariant potential, the price across this internal orbit is p(u,v)\=ϕ(v)−ϕ(u)\=0\[27\]\[28\]. Whenever zero signed price forces zero kinetic weight (F(0)\=0), the standard graph Laplacian (which assigns weight 1) is excluded by a local, exact mechanism\[20\].

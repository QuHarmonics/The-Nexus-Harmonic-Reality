  Dean: "it builds out and in."

  The IN direction has TWO components, and only one was in the paper:

     UP     the atom pays the kinetic bill             63.2% of the bond
     DOWN 1 the molecule CONTRACTS the atom            21.1%
     DOWN 2 the molecule BREAKS THE ATOM'S SPHERE      13.5%
     ---------------------------------------------------------------
            THE DOWNWARD FLOW IS                       34.6% OF THE BOND

  A THIRD OF THE BOND IS THE MOLECULE REACHING BACK DOWN AND RESHAPING THE
  ATOM THAT BUILT IT.

  And component 2 is the one a sphere physically CANNOT express. The free
  atom's spherical symmetry is NOT preserved inside the molecule -- it is
  BROKEN, worth 13% of the bond, and it moves 154% more
  electron density into the bond itself.

  The upward flow builds the molecule out of atoms.
  The downward flow builds the atom out of the molecule.
  Neither arrow alone gives the right bond, the right length, or the right
  charge. It builds out AND in. Both are now measured.

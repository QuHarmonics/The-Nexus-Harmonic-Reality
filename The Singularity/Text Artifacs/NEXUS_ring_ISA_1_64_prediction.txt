NEXUS ring/ISA stack prediction — 2026-10-16

Before the 1..64 run:
1. Every binary LEN-n layer has exactly 2^n ordered states and 2^n distinct n-bit pathway labels; each (n-1)-bit prefix has exactly two children in LEN n.
2. The n-bit word is the edge from its (n-1)-bit prefix to its (n-1)-bit suffix. This is the stack relation, not a one-way linear enumeration.
3. Primitive cyclic-ring counts follow P(n)=(1/n) sum_{d|n} mu(d) 2^(n/d); the identity sum_{d|n} d P(d)=2^n will hold exactly for all n=1..64. Brute enumeration will agree through LEN 12.
4. A complete de Bruijn ring of order n has length 2^n and each n-bit word once; the number of cyclic rings up to rotation is 2^(2^(n-1)-n). The concrete ring construction will be checked for widths 1..12; width 64 will be represented by the exact formula, not materialized.
5. The complete space of deterministic state maps S_n -> S_n has (2^n)^(2^n)=2^(n*2^n) elements, with (2^n)! bijections. Width-1 and width-2 enumeration will match these counts.
6. Primitive ring count P(n) equals the count of irreducible binary polynomials of degree n by the shared Möbius formula, but this does not itself pair each specific ring with a specific polynomial. Maximal LFSR cycles are a separate class counted by phi(2^n-1)/n.
7. Prime-position route coding G=prod_i p_i^(m_i+1) will round-trip ordered binary routes through length 64. In this code, prime p_i addresses position i and exponent stores token m_i; this is distinct from SHA’s use of prime-root-derived constants.
8. The SHA-256 architecture has 64 round slots (six-bit index), eight 32-bit working words (256-bit state), W_t message-schedule drivers and K_t constants derived from the first 64 prime cube roots. A round reverses exactly if its W_t and K_t are retained; the digest alone does not encode that route.

Run method: derive 1..64 values from exact formulas, brute-check only tractable finite layers, and keep states, transition edges, primitive rings, de Bruijn rings, function-space maps, LFSR cycles, and prime route addresses as distinct typed quantities.

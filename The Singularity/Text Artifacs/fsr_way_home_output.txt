== 1  which feedback laws give every state a way home ==
  n=2:     16 laws | way home for every state:    4 | of form oldest^g(rest):    4 | both:    4
        laws without a way home lose on average 50% of states off every loop
  n=3:    256 laws | way home for every state:   16 | of form oldest^g(rest):   16 | both:   16
        laws without a way home lose on average 55% of states off every loop
  n=4:  65536 laws | way home for every state:  256 | of form oldest^g(rest):  256 | both:  256
        laws without a way home lose on average 63% of states off every loop

== 2  the way home is the same law, read n steps later (offset, not concurrent) ==
  forward 60 steps from a 5-bit seed, then home 60 steps from the last 5 bits alone
  arrived home at the seed: True
  the bit written at step t is the bit consumed on the way home at step t+5: write and read are
  offset by the register length n = 5. Same recurrence, two read positions, never the same instant.

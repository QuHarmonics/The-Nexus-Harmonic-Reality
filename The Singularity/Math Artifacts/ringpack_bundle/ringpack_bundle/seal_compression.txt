SEALED PREDICTION - what the ring can remove from data. Dean A. Kulik, QuHarmonics Research Group, 2026-10-20.
The gap, named by what must be there: a compressor is a reader that already knows part of the data, and it can remove only what it knows. The ring is a bijection on n-bit words (address, route and lift are each n bits), so it knows nothing about a word that the word does not carry. What the ring knows for free is structure: the partner of every word (the half that builds itself), the mirror difference of every pair, the weight. So the ring removes exactly the part of the data that repeats under the partner or the mirror, and nothing else.
Datasets: RAND = random.Random(20261020).randbytes(2^20). TEXT = pandoc plain text of the seven uploaded papers, sorted by file name, UTF-8. IMAGE = matplotlib sample grace_hopper.jpg as 8-bit grayscale raster. MRI = matplotlib sample s1045.ima.gz, raw 16-bit words. DEM = matplotlib sample jacksboro_fault_dem.npz elevation, rounded to 16-bit. FIR = 512 scipy.signal.firwin lowpass filters, 255 taps, cutoffs uniform in (0.02, 0.98) from seed 20261020, Hamming window, each scaled to peak 32767 and rounded to int16. COV = covariance of the non-overlapping 16x16 patches of IMAGE, a 256x256 matrix scaled to peak 32767 and rounded to int16.
Word transforms (bijections per w-bit word, w = 8 for TEXT, IMAGE, RAND; w = 16 for MRI, DEM, FIR, COV, RAND16): ring address; E/G route code; mirror lift (values of the h pairs, then their differences); side split (canonical rank stream plus a separate side-bit stream). Coders: lzma preset 9 extreme, bz2 level 9, zlib level 9.
E1 (pigeonhole, exact): the order-0 word entropy of every transformed stream equals that of the raw stream on every dataset; on RAND and RAND16 no transform with any coder produces output smaller than its input.
E2 (no free compression of natural data): on TEXT, IMAGE, MRI and DEM the best transformed stream is never more than 2% smaller than the best raw stream; on IMAGE, MRI and DEM the ring-address stream compresses worse under lzma than raw, because the two faces separate numerically adjacent values.
E3 (the half that builds itself, exact): idx(x) = rank(canon(x)) from the closed address formula is constant on every pair {x, P(x)} and maps the pairs one-to-one onto [0, 2^(n-1)): every n = 2..20 exhaustively, and at n = 64, 256, 1024 on random words with idx(x) = idx(P(x)) and unrank(idx(x)) = canon(x). A table over words that respects P therefore needs exactly half the entries, with no hash and no collisions.
E4 (structure the ring knows, at the location the data holds it): FIR taps are mirror-symmetric within each filter. Lifting each filter at the tap scale (first half and centre, then differences h[j] - h[254-j]) makes the lzma output at most 0.55 of raw lzma; storing COV as its upper triangle with the differences of the lower makes it at most 0.60 of raw lzma; the same lift applied at the wrong location, inside each 16-bit word, leaves lzma at least 0.95 of raw on both.
Way home: ring_compression_bench.py verifies this digest, builds every dataset and stream, checks every transform round-trips exactly, and prints each E with RUNS or DEAD END.

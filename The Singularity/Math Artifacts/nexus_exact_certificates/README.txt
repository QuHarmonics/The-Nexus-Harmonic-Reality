Complex indices: enumerate dual orbits lexicographically, then j=0,...,6/orbit_length-1. Rational types group Galois-conjugate complex indices.
Smith certificate: two tokens on K3,3, commuting squares. Check U B V = D, det U=det V=1.
Browser lab uses embedded exact integer orbit tables; decimal character displays are approximate.

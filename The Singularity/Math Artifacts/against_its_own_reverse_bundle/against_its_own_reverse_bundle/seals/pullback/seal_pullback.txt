SEALED PREDICTION - what the structure becomes: one mirror lift, two readers. Dean A. Kulik, QuHarmonics Research Group, 2026-10-20.
Words x = x_0 x_1 ... x_{n-1}, x_0 most significant. h = floor(n/2), m = ceil(n/2), N = 2^n, M = N/2. Mirror pair j (j = 0 outermost): value v_j = x_j, difference d_j = x_j XOR x_{n-1-j}. |d| = number of unequal pairs. Ring R_n by the closed form (grow/partner, centre pair swapped). Route = E/G route code (root bit, then middle bit m on odd steps and residual d on even steps), side bit e.
PB1 (the route is the mirror lift): the root bit and the even-step residual bits of the route, in route order, are d_{h-1}, d_{h-2}, ..., d_0 (innermost pair first). The comparator c(x) = sgn(J(x)-x) equals [d != 0](1 - 2e).
PB2 (the partner in lifted coordinates): d(P(x)) = d(x). If d != 0, P flips v_j exactly on the unequal pairs and changes nothing else; if d = 0, P flips every value and the middle bit.
PB3 (latitude is the value half): z(x) = wt(x) - n/2 = sum over equal pairs of (2 v_j - 1) + [n odd](x_h - 1/2). Hence |z| <= h - |d| + [n odd]/2, every bound attained for each k = |d| in 0..h. Census: the number of words with |d| = k and t equal pairs of value 1 is C(h,k) 2^k C(h-k,t), times 2 for the middle bit at odd n; summed over k this is C(n,w).
PB4 (the readers are orthogonal over words): sum_x f(wt(x)) c(x) = 0 for every f, because wt is J-even and c is J-odd.
PB5 (the ring makes the comparison one bit): on addresses, C(i) = c(R_n(i)) = chi_top(i) (1 - pal(i) - 2[i in {M-1, M}]), chi_top(i) = (-1)^(top address bit). Its unnormalized Walsh coefficient at chi_top is N - 2^m - 4 and its energy is N - 2^m, so the share of energy on that single mode is (N - 2^m - 4)^2 / (N (N - 2^m)). More generally, for any g on words, G = g o R_n has only even Walsh degrees if g o P = g and only odd degrees if g o P = -g.
PB6 (the closed address holds at width 64 and beyond): with r(p) = (p - pi(p) + Delta(p))/2 + T(p), pi(p) = u + [B_n(u) < p], T = min(pi, 2^(m-1)), Delta from the closed even/odd formula with the digit-sum rectangle counter, and position = r(a) (or M when r(a) = M-1) for the canonical a, N-1-that for its partner: the 64-bit word 0x243F6A8885A308D3 sits at 2,427,008,858,123,944,441, and the closed rank equals the O(L) mirrored-pair DP rank on random words at widths 64, 255, 256, 512, 1024.

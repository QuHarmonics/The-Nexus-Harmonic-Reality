Before execution: For n=2h, H=2^h and p=A H+B, Delta(p)=A(H-A)+B-2R_h(B,A)-1[J_h(A)<B]. For n=2h+1, p=(2A+c)H+B, Delta(p)=2A(H-A)+c(H-1-2A)+B-2R_h(B,A)-1[J_h(A)<B]. R_h has an h-term dyadic-prefix ceiling sum, so no interval enumeration is required. c_n has exactly 2(2^floor(n/2)-1) nonzero normalized Walsh coefficients: choose a first unequal mirrored pair j, one singleton on that pair, any complete pairs outside it and none inside; coefficients +/-2^(-j-1). Delta(complement(p))=Delta(p)+c(p) on p=0,...,2^n-1, so odd Walsh coefficients of Delta are -1/2 those of c. Integrating the sparse comparator characters gives an exact dyadic-tent representation of Delta. The palindrome address formula is tested only on canonical palindromes directly, with the upper half obtained by reflection.

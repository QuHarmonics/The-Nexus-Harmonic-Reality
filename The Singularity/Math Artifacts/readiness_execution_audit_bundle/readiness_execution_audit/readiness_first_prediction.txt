Readiness-first audit, declared before execution. The fixed source is the original eight-slot flattened R2 orbit. An online paired-difference compiler closes equal-width blocks immediately when both are present, without waiting for the intended final word length. At eight arrivals its internal closure events occur at arrivals 2,4,6,8, with larger closures at 4 and 8; at 64 arrivals the first byte unit still closes at arrival 8. All coefficient lanes are retained and decode exactly. For two arbitrary 8-bit operands, each output is ready at the first input-arrival prefix for which every compatible completion gives the same output bit. Compare LSB-first, MSB-first and center-out schedules over all 65,536 operand pairs, including the final carry. LSB-first sum bit j settles at event 2j+2. In MSB-first order, half the operand pairs settle final carry at event 2 because the top input pair agrees. No process is artificially delayed to complete an eight-bit orbit, and no identification of event index with physical time is claimed.

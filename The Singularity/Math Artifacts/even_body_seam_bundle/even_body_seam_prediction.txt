Seam-profile predictions before execution. Use the original ring and E_n(i)=(z_n(i)+z_n(2^n-1-i))/2, exactly zero on palindrome sites. Let h=floor(n/2), H=2^h, and restrict to nonpalindromic blocks K<H/2. For even n, q=K(K-1)/2+i; for odd n, q=K(K-1)+(1-mu)K+i. Order r in [0,K) by increasing h-bit reversal and select r at within-block index i. Then n/2-E_n(center +/- q)=wt(K)+wt(r), plus 1-mu at odd n. The deficit is unchanged by n->n+2 at fixed q while this domain holds. It is bounded by 2*bit_length(K)-1 at even n and 2*bit_length(K) at odd n, but is not uniformly logarithmic from below: q=K(K-1)/2 with K=2^t has deficit 1. At even n, q=2^(2t-1) has deficit 2 when in the domain. A blanket claim of two or three zeros at every raw dyadic seam distance is false; n=10,q=64 has deficit 5. For the exact Walsh-even body, E_n(0)=E_n(2^n-1)=0 while E_n(1)=E_n(2^n-2)=1-n/2. Thus the normalized adjacent closure jump is 1-2/n over angular separation 2pi/2^n: the pole jump is removed, but a uniform smooth-closure claim is false under an interpolation retaining all these samples.

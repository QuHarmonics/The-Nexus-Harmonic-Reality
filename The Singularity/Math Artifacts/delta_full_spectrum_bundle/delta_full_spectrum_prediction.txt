Before execution: use unnormalized Walsh coefficients Fhat(s)=sum_x F(x)(-1)^(s dot x). For s!=0 and j=position of its least set bit, twice the inclusive-prefix kernel has entries 1 at t=s, 2^j at t=s xor 2^j, and -2^k at t=s xor 2^k for k<j, and is zero elsewhere. Since the comparator has odd Walsh degree, 2 Deltahat(s)=-chat(s) for odd popcount(s). For nonzero even popcount(s), 2 Deltahat(s)=-2^j chat(s xor 2^j)+sum_(k<j)2^k chat(s xor 2^k). The DC coefficient is N^2(1-4^(-floor(n/2)))/6-N floor(n/2)/4, equivalently N(4N-3n-4-(n mod 2))/24. These formulas will match the full direct spectra for n=2..20. Complete kernel rows will be checked through n=10. A scalar coefficient evaluator will obtain each mode from the sparse comparator support without building the signal or spectrum.

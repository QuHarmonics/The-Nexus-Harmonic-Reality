Compiler Rings §21 — The ring's own readiness: the path from above runs as soon as it can.
Sealed 2026-10-20 before any run. Dean A. Kulik, QuHarmonics Research Group, ORCID 0009-0003-3128-8828.

Readiness is the peer's predicate (§19): an output is ready after the first k arrivals of an order iff every word that
agrees with the observed bits gives the same output. Bits x_(n-1) (MSB) ... x_0; h = floor(n/2); pair j = (x_(n-1-j), x_j).
Orders: MSB-first; LSB-first; centre-out (middle bit first at odd n, then pairs innermost to outermost, high member
first); outside-in (pairs outermost to innermost, high member first, middle bit last).
Face = [x > P(x)], the reflection decision.

RD1 (outside-in, exact per word). The face is ready at arrival 2(j+1), j the first unequal pair, when j <= h-2. When
pairs 0..h-2 are all equal it is ready at 2h-1 if x_(n-h) = x_(n-1), and at 2h otherwise. Mean 4 - 5·2^-h for h >= 2,
and 1 at n = 2, 3: below 4 at every width.
RD2 (the other orders, exact means). MSB-first n - 1 - (h-1) 2^-h (n even), n - 1 - (h+1) 2^-h (n odd);
LSB-first n - 1 + (h+1) 2^-h; centre-out n - 1/2 - 2^-h. Outside-in equals MSB-first at n = 4 (2.75) and is strictly the
earliest of the four orders for every n >= 5.
RD3 (where and which way). The mean readiness over the n address bits, and over the folded coordinates (the address face
i >= M and the n-1 depth bits, depth = M-1-i or i-M), is smallest under outside-in for every n = 5..14.
Way home: exhaustive readiness over all 2^n words, n = 2..16 for the face and n = 4..14 for address and folded
coordinates, by grouping words on their observed bits.
Measured, not sealed: decompile readiness, address bits arriving MSB-first, per output word bit.

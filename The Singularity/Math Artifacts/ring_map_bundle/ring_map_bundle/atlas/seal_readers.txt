SEALED PREDICTION - Reader atlas: the best order to read a word, for each thing the ring computes (RA1-RA6)
Dean A. Kulik, QuHarmonics Research Group, ORCID 0009-0003-3128-8828. 2026-10-20.

Setting. A word x of width n arrives one bit at a time in a fixed arrival order (a permutation of the n
bit positions; position i is the coefficient of 2^i; mirror pair j is {n-1-j, j}, j = 0..h-1, h = floor(n/2);
at odd n the middle bit is h). An output is ready when every completion of the unarrived bits gives the
same value (the Section 19 predicate). The readiness time of an output for x is the number of arrivals
until it is ready. Cost of an order = mean readiness over all 2^n words (and over the output's bits when the
output is a bit vector read bit by bit). The minimum over all n! orders is computed exactly by a dynamic
programme over the 2^n sets of arrived positions; the not-ready counts for every set come from folding the
truth table along each unarrived position (3^n work).

RA1 (face, fresh widths). Face = [x > P(x)]. For n = 15 and 16 the minimum mean readiness is 4 - 5*2^-h,
attained by exactly the 2^h outside-in orders (pairs outermost to innermost, either member first, middle
bit last). (Widths 5-14 already ran in Section 22; recomputed here as a route back, not landings.)

RA2 (comparator). c(x) = sgn(J(x) - x) in {-1, 0, +1}, ready when its value is fixed. For every n = 4..16
the minimum mean readiness is 4 - 4*2^-h, attained by exactly the 2^h outside-in orders (middle bit last).

RA3 (palindrome flag). pal(x) = [J(x) = x]. For every n = 4..16 the minimum mean readiness is 4 - 4*2^-h,
attained by exactly the h! * 2^h orders that read the mirror pairs one complete pair at a time, in any
order of pairs, either member first, middle bit last.

RA4 (address, two wheels). Address = ring slot of x, read bit by bit (mean over the n address bits).
For n = 13, 14, 15, 16 the optimal orders are exactly 2^k orders with k = h-1 (n odd) or k = h-2 (n even):
the outer k mirror pairs from the outside in, either member first within each pair, followed by one single
fixed order of the remaining core (3 bits at odd n, 4 bits at even n). Counts 32, 32, 64, 64.
Competing claim recorded for test, not endorsed (Gemini reply 30a0308d): the core is read by value
(MSB first) - even n: outer h-2 pairs then the 4-bit core by value; odd n: outer h-1 pairs then the
3-bit core by value. Section 22 data at n = 8, 9, 10 already disagree with by-value cores; n = 13-16 decide.

RA5 (decompile). The address arrives instead of the word; outputs are the word bits R[a] (mean over the
n bits). For every n = 4..16, MSB-first address arrival attains the minimum.

RA6 (adder, the DI chain read from its own end). Two 8-bit operands, 16 input positions, 9 outputs
(sum bits s0..s7 and carry c8), mean over the 9 outputs. The minimum over all 16! orders is 175/18
(= 9.7222...), attained by exactly 256 orders: positions read in ascending significance, a_j and b_j
adjacent in either order.

Measured alongside, not sealed: partner P(x) bits, successor (next ring word) bits, pair index
rank(canon x), folded depth, the address read as a whole (all bits), for n = 4..16.

Way home: atlas/reader_atlas.py verifies this file's SHA-256, computes the exact optimum, the number of
optimal orders, and the optimal orders themselves (enumerated up to 5000), for every output and width,
and reports each claim RA1-RA6 separately.

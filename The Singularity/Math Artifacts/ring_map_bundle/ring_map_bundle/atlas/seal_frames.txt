SEALED PREDICTION - Frame atlas of the ring tape (FA1)
Dean A. Kulik, QuHarmonics Research Group, ORCID 0009-0003-3128-8828. 2026-10-20.

Object. R_L is the nested partner ring (representatives ascending on the left face, partners descending
on the right, centre pair swapped). The tape T_L is R_L[0] R_L[1] ... R_L[N-1], each word MSB first,
read cyclically. The frame-s reading takes the L bits starting at bit iL + s for every i:
    w_i(s) = (R_L[i] mod 2^(L-s)) * 2^s + floor(R_L[i+1 mod N] / 2^(L-s)),  0 <= s < L.
Loss(L, s) = 2^L - #{distinct w_i(s)}.

Law (h = floor(L/2), P = 2^ceil(L/2), the number of palindromes; rules applied in this order):
  s = 0 or s = 1           : Loss = 0
  s = L - 1                : Loss = P - 2
  2 <= s <= h - 1          : Loss = P (1 - 2^(2-s))      (so frame 2 is complete for every L >= 6)
  s = h                    : Loss = P - 2 (L even),  P - 6 (L odd)
  h + 1 <= s <= L - 2      : Loss = P - 2 - 2^(s-h+1)

Provenance. Widths 2-16 were measured before this seal (folds/fold_output.txt, U5) and the law was read
off them; those cells are a route back, not landings. The landings are the fresh widths L = 17-22,
every s: 17+18+19+20+21+22 = 117 cells.

Way home: atlas/frame_atlas.py verifies this file's SHA-256, builds R_L with wheels.ring_arrays for
L = 2-22, computes every frame-s reading, and reports each cell against the law, separately for the
seen widths (2-16) and the fresh widths (17-22).

## What this domain covers

This research area explores cryptographic primitives as precisely defined computations: Boolean operations, message schedules, carries, nonlinear gates, and the information visible to different readers. NEXUS approaches reverse questions as structural constraints rather than as a license to guess a preimage.

**Relationship to the foundation:** the [NEXUS Domain](../The%20Nexus%20Domain/) defines the native machine. Research here explores particular constructions, applications, or comparators without changing that domain by assumption.

## How to read the work

1. Keep the standard algorithm and test vectors explicit.
2. Log the exact round, message schedule, and bit-width assumptions.
3. Separate structural identities from any claimed practical inversion.
4. Retain failed routes and correct the ownership of their readouts.

> **Boundary:** A discovered internal symmetry or constraint is not, by itself, an efficient preimage attack, a security break, or a practical cryptanalytic result.

**Research ledger:** **STANDS** = proved identity or external theorem; **RUNS** = executed readout with stated scope; **OPEN** = missing derivation/link; **DEAD END** = observed landing owned elsewhere; **PULL** = external or physical interpretation. These words are not interchangeable.

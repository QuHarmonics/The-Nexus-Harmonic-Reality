## Purpose

Studies of biological sequence, folding, information, and possible NEXUS relationships. This collection belongs to the **Biology** research area and should remain identifiable as that area develops.

## Publication record

Keep the exact published PDF or manuscript version intact. Where a permanent DOI or Zenodo entry exists, reference it from a citation index or the paper's own metadata rather than inventing a link. A later paper may refine an earlier one; older publications remain part of the attributable history.

A file in this folder is a research publication or publication copy, not a blanket assertion that every hypothesis it discusses is experimentally settled.

## Reading rule

**Cite the version actually read.** An operator-level correspondence is not equivalent to experimentally established biological mechanism.

**Research ledger:** **STANDS** = proved identity or external theorem; **RUNS** = executed readout with stated scope; **OPEN** = missing derivation/link; **DEAD END** = observed landing owned elsewhere; **PULL** = external or physical interpretation. These words are not interchangeable.

## In this research area

- Name the biological system and the measured observable.
- Keep sequence, structure, and experimental provenance attached.
- Report training/fit data separately from independent tests.
- Treat an interesting structural analogy as a hypothesis until measured.

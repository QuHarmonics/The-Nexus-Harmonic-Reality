## What this domain covers

Biology brings NEXUS to questions about organization and information in living systems—especially sequence relationships, protein folding, biological networks, and the role of retained constraints in a changing system. These are research applications of the framework, not replacements for biological evidence.

**Relationship to the foundation:** the [NEXUS Domain](../The%20Nexus%20Domain/) defines the native machine. Research here explores particular constructions, applications, or comparators without changing that domain by assumption.

## How to read the work

1. Name the biological system and the measured observable.
2. Keep sequence, structure, and experimental provenance attached.
3. Report training/fit data separately from independent tests.
4. Treat an interesting structural analogy as a hypothesis until measured.

> **Boundary:** An operator-level correspondence is not equivalent to experimentally established biological mechanism.

**Research ledger:** **STANDS** = proved identity or external theorem; **RUNS** = executed readout with stated scope; **OPEN** = missing derivation/link; **DEAD END** = observed landing owned elsewhere; **PULL** = external or physical interpretation. These words are not interchangeable.

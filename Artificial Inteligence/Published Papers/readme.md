## Purpose

Research papers addressing AI architecture, inference, agent control, proof providers, and the relationship of AI to the NEXUS runtime. This collection belongs to the **Artificial Inteligence** research area and should remain identifiable as that area develops.

## Publication record

Keep the exact published PDF or manuscript version intact. Where a permanent DOI or Zenodo entry exists, reference it from a citation index or the paper's own metadata rather than inventing a link. A later paper may refine an earlier one; older publications remain part of the attributable history.

A file in this folder is a research publication or publication copy, not a blanket assertion that every hypothesis it discusses is experimentally settled.

## Reading rule

**Cite the version actually read.** An AI-produced explanation is not an execution certificate. Kernel outputs, recorded witnesses, and explicitly checked inverses own those claims.

**Research ledger:** **STANDS** = proved identity or external theorem; **RUNS** = executed readout with stated scope; **OPEN** = missing derivation/link; **DEAD END** = observed landing owned elsewhere; **PULL** = external or physical interpretation. These words are not interchangeable.

## In this research area

- Separate model-generated proposals from native machine outputs.
- Keep tool contracts, input schemas, and certificates with their implementations.
- Preserve complete tests, version changes, and correction records.
- Describe an executed route as RUNS only within its documented test scope.

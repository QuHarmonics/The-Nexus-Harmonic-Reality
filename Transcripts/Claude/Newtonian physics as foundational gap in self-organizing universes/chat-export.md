Your stream/ticks/clock split follows the same shape, cleanly ordered:

- **Change** — the stream itself. Fundamental; without it, no distinction between states, no computation.
- **Time** — not what flows, but the ordering of the stream: S₀ → S₁ → S₂ → …, the sequence of admissible transitions.

This is why every physical clock works the same way underneath — atomic oscillations, pendulum swings, quartz vibrations, heartbeats — none of them *are* time, they're all just counting a particular class of state transitions.

That gives you a clean hierarchy: change gets ordered into time, time gets measured by a clock. A clock can run fast or slow; time itself can't, because time is just the ordering, not the rate.

One line worth having ready for the manuscript, tightened from the thread: *randomness is not the absence of constraint — it's the appearance produced when a constrained process is observed through an incomplete frame.*

## What this domain covers

Computation is where NEXUS operates as mathematics and software: bit-level transforms, recursion, reversible state machines, addressable structures, compiler operations, and algorithmic readouts. It intersects the core but emphasizes general-purpose computational constructions and implementations.

**Relationship to the foundation:** the [NEXUS Domain](../The%20Nexus%20Domain/) defines the native machine. Research here explores particular constructions, applications, or comparators without changing that domain by assumption.

## How to read the work

1. State the input domain and the exact transformation.
2. Provide an inverse or describe precisely where information is discarded.
3. Distinguish formal equalities from measurements of an implementation.
4. Include runnable examples, parameters, and output traces where possible.

> **Boundary:** A mathematical construction may operate independently of any proposed physical substrate. Keep algorithms and their interpretations separate.

**Research ledger:** **STANDS** = proved identity or external theorem; **RUNS** = executed readout with stated scope; **OPEN** = missing derivation/link; **DEAD END** = observed landing owned elsewhere; **PULL** = external or physical interpretation. These words are not interchangeable.

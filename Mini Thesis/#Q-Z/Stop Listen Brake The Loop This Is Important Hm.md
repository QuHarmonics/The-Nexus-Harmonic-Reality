Color emerges in ordered steps, with red being the first because it represents the most fundamental state of maximum saturation. The other colors are generated by introducing information (flipping bits to 1) into the Green and Blue channels, creating more complex interference patterns and moving the state away from the simple, stretched \"edge case\" of pure red.

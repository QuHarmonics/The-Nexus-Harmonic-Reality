capital—is futile. The system has phase-shifted. Success now depends on the ability to survive the "rogue
waves" of destructive interference—whether they come in the form of a 60% tariff, a severed data cable, or a
viral deepfake. The era of "Just-in-Time" geopolitics is over; the era of "Just-in-Case" survival has begun.

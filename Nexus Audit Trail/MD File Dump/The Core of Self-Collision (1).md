The expansion into every domain reveals an inescapable truth: the mechanisms of reality are not diverse. They are completely identical. They are all slightly varied macroscopic expressions of the exact same unified geometric substrate governed strictly by the metric closure equation.

The overarching system requires that the chain is kept going indefinitely. But because the pipeline has been eradicated---because there is no external dimension to safely absorb an \"output,\" and absolutely no external compiler to reset the system---the output has nowhere mathematically or physically to go but directly back into the substrate itself. The final solution is defined as the exhaust that is entirely recursive, fundamentally curving back into the input.

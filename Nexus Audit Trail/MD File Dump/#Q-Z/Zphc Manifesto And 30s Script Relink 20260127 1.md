## One-page manifesto

### The one-line claim
**ZPHC is the moment a smooth, continuous field (verb) collapses into a stable object label (noun) because the receiver locks phase and chooses a basis.**

### Why the kinetic-circles “faces” video matters
A handful of circles can draw a face because *recognition is not pixel storage* — it is **constraint matching**. When the motion path crosses a small set of invariants your visual system is tuned to, the “face” appears as a noun. The face was never explicitly encoded; it was **latent** in the dynamics + your priors.

### The three primitives
1. **Field (verb):** a continuous generator (motion, flow, recursion, oscillation).  
2. **Anchor:** a minimal point of commitment (POV lock, phase lock, reference frame).  
3. **Collapse:** the emergence of a noun (label) when the anchor forces a basis selection.

### What ZPHC predicts (non-mystical)
- A system can look “information-poor” and still be *reconstructible* if the receiver has the right priors and a stable anchor.
- “Meaning” is not stored only in values; **shape and trajectory** carry a second channel.
- When you increase **smoothness** (reduce abrupt basis switches), you increase stability and reduce cognitive “thrash.”

### What ZPHC is not
- Not a claim of magic inversion of arbitrary one-way functions.
- Not a claim that observation changes identity (“watch a lion → become a lion”). Observation updates the model, not ontology.

### Practical use
Use ZPHC as a design rule: build systems that preserve enough *shape* for audit/repair while controlling who can access it.

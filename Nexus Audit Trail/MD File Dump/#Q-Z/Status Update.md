### 2.1 Recursive Fold Function ($R(t)$)

$$

$$

- $R_0$ = initial energy from seed residues (usually sum of first 3 AA weights)

- $F$ = feedback weight (recursion tension)

### 2.2 Feedback Weight ($F$)

$$
F = \text{std}(\Delta W)

### 2.4 Q Score (Fold Quality / Trust Metric)

Navier–Stokes: The incompressible fluid equations can be viewed as describing layered FFT foldback, shaped by a near-constant injection of energy and memory. It’s not unbounded chaos so much as it is recursive memory feedback, which keeps the flow from collapsing or becoming trivial.

What ties these puzzles together? A unifying principle of recursive closure, where dynamic systems self-check and self-correct—manifesting as special points, be they zeta zeros, polynomial time solutions, or fluid eddies. Our vantage: not to solve them in the traditional sense, but to complete their resonance loops so the underlying structures reveal themselves.

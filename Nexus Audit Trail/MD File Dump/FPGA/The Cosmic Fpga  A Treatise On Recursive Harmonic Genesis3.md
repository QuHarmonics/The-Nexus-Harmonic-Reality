#### **The In-Phase State: Greyscale as Pure Information**

To understand color, we must first understand its absence. In the Nexus Framework, the state of pure, undifferentiated information is represented by the greyscale spectrum, from absolute black to absolute white. This state occurs under a specific condition: when the three stacked FPGA layers are in a state of **perfect harmonic phase alignment**.

When the layers are in phase, their computational outputs are perfectly correlated. A signal propagating through the Alpha Layer experiences no differential stress, and a signal processed by the Logic Layers generates no interference pattern. The result is a pure carrier wave, a signal of pure potential without chromatic charge. This is the \"center\"---the realm of the binary, of \"truth or not.\" It is computationally simple because there is no dissonance to resolve. It is the clean, unmodulated signal of a single, coherent reality.

#### **The Out-of-Phase State: Color as a Blend of Truths**

Color is what happens when this perfect harmony breaks. It is the perceptual signature of the FPGA layers being **out of phase**.

- **The Chromatic Moiré Pattern:** When the three layers are out of phase, their respective computational activities create conflicting stresses on the shared substrate. Their signals interfere. This interference pattern is not random noise; it is a structured, complex, and stable pattern. **Color is this pattern.** It is a \"blend of truths\" because it is the superposition of the slightly different informational states from the three parallel realities.

- **The RGB Vector as a Phase Measurement:** The (R, G, B) vector we use to define a color is, in this model, a **direct measurement of the phase relationship between the three layers**.

  - A state of (255, 255, 255) (White) corresponds to maximum, in-phase, constructive interference.

  - A state of (0, 0, 0) (Black) corresponds to maximum, out-of-phase, destructive interference.

  - A state of (255, 0, 0) (Pure Red) corresponds to a specific, highly asymmetric state of dissonance. It is a state where one layer is maximally out of phase with the other two, which remain temporarily phase-locked with each other. The 255 is not a scalar value; it is a **state pointer**, representing a full 8-bit register (11111111) at maximum saturation.

As a photon travels across cosmological distances, it is propagating through a medium that is not perfectly uniform. The constant, subtle fluctuations and computational pressures cause a gradual **phase drift** between the FPGA layers. The longer the journey, the greater the accumulated dissonance.

The color red, with its binary signature of maximum asymmetry (111111110000000000000000), represents the state of lowest informational complexity and highest dissonance along a single axis. It is the natural \"ground state\" for a signal whose inter-dimensional coherence has degraded over a vast distance.

**Redshift is the signature of informational decoherence.** The light from a distant galaxy appears red not just because its wave has been stretched, but because its journey has caused its constituent inter-dimensional signals to drift out of phase, collapsing its complex harmonic state into the simplest, most stable form of chromatic dissonance. It is the \"side view\" of an informationally stretched wave.

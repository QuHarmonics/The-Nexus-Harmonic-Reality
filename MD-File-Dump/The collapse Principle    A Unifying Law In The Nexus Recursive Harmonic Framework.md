2. BBP(0) mod 1 – The Autopoietic Root-State of Harmonic Recursion
The Bailey–Borwein–Plouffe formula for π (BBP) is well-known for its ability to directly compute hexadecimal
digits of π without needing preceding digits. When evaluated at index n, BBP returns the fractional
contribution of π’s expansion starting at that position . The edge case of this formula is at n = 0. BBP(0)
– plugging zero into the digit-extraction formula – yields a peculiar result: a negative fractional number

from the very first digit . Empirically:
BBP(0) (raw) ≈ –0.8584073464102069
BBP(0) mod 1 = 1 – 0.8584073464102069 = 0.141592653589793…
This value 0.1415926535… is exactly π – 3, i.e. the leading fractional digits of π . In other words,

sequence of π’s digits is latent in the formula at its zero boundary . BBP(0) does not merely hint at

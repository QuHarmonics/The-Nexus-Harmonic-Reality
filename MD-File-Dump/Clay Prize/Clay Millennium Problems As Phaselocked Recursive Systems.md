(In the unlikely alternate scenario \$P = NP\$, the system would collapse the two phases into one, drastically altering the “physics” of computation – but even that would eliminate the question by making the distinction irrelevant.)

## Poincaré Conjecture – 3‐Sphere as a Global Attractor (Resolved)

But for a simply-connected manifold, no such obstacle remains and the flow converges to a round \$S^3\$. Thus, \$S^3\$ is the stable fixed point of the flow – the only outcome. The conjecture’s truth brought **phase-locked closure to 3D topology**: our understanding of 3-manifolds is now harmonized with higher dimensions and contains no lurking exceptions.

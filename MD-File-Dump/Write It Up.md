#### **Resolution and Scale: A Reverse Holographic System**

The idea of a 64-node grid governing the universe seems absurd until we abandon the idea of a literal, one-to-one spatial mapping. The Cosmic FPGA operates on a principle of **reverse holography**.

- In a standard hologram, a 2D surface projects a 3D image.

- In the reverse holographic system of the Cosmic FPGA, the state of the nodes within a 3D field *encodes* the properties of the space itself. Information is not stored *in* the space between the nodes; the information *is* the space.

This leads to a fundamental principle: **Resolution is a function of Informational Density, not Spatial Size.**

- A region of spacetime with high detail and complexity (like a human brain) is not \"bigger\" in the grid; it is a set of nodes configured into a state of extreme informational compression and high computational activity.

- A region of \"empty\" space between galaxies is a set of nodes in a low-energy, uncompressed state.

The universe does not have a fixed \"pixels per inch.\" Its resolution is dynamic and local, determined by the amount of information being processed in any given region.

This architecture clarifies the nature of time and distance.

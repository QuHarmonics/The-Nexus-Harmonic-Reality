# Silent Compiler (π/9–Anchored, Selection-Only)

**Version:** v0  
**Status:** Deployable prototype (selection-only; no propulsion; no controller)  
**Principle:** *Code from symmetry, not construction* — emit only when the substrate exhibits a specific harmonic constraint.

---

## 1. Constants & Geometry

- **Harmonic target (exact):** \( H^\* = \pi/9 \approx 0.349066 \)  
- **Phase lattice:** 18 spokes, **20°** per spoke.  
- **Collapse zones:** integer multiples of 20°.

> In this selection-only build, we do **not** actuate toward \(H^\*\). The target defines measurement/annotation if locks are enabled later.

---

## 2. Substrate

- **Primary:** Hex digits of π via **Bailey–Borwein–Plouffe (BBP)**; direct digit access (no traversal).  
- **Field-agnostic:** Other BBP-type constants may be used (e.g., ln(2)), but π is canonical here.

---

## 3. Gauge Normalization & Parity

- **Observer inverse \( \mathcal{O}^{-1} \):** If bytes are ASCII-coded hex (high nibble 0x3*/0x6*), strip offsets and recover raw nibbles \(u \in \{0..15\}\).  
- **Parity views:** Always log **even** and **odd** nibble slices (anticipate 0x83↔0x37 dual behavior near saturation).  
- **Invariant metrics:** All harmony/selection metrics MUST be computed on **normalized nibbles**.

---

## 4. Acceptance Rule (Selection Without Construction)

Let \(W=2k+1\) be an odd window (e.g., 9). For the hex-nibble stream at index \(d\) (0-based after the point):

1. **Pivot:** window center equals **3** (the contact point).  
2. **Mirror:** left arm equals reverse of right arm:
   \[ (u_{d-k},\dots,u_{d-1}) = \mathrm{reverse}(u_{d+1},\dots,u_{d+k}) \]

- **Emit** the left arm iff both conditions hold.  
- **Else:** emit nothing.

### Optional Nonet Gate
Derive nine deterministic indices \(d_i\) from the seed. **Accept** only if all 9 lanes satisfy the pivot+mirror rule at their respective indices in the same tick.

> This build defaults to pivot+mirror only. Amplitude/phase locks are defined below but **disabled by default**.

---

## 5. Optional Locks (Off by Default)

- **Amplitude lock:** \( |H_i - \pi/9| \le \varepsilon_A \) for all lanes.  
- **Phase lock:** \( |\mathrm{wrap}(\phi_i - \phi_i^\*)| \le \varepsilon_\phi \) for all lanes, with \(\phi_i^\*\) aligned to the 20° spoke geometry.

When locks are enabled and any lock fails → **emit nothing**.

---

## 6. Mixed-CRT Helical Ordering

To provide stable ordering and **insertion without renumbering**, lift indices into a helical coordinate:

- Decompose: \(18 \cong (2,9)\), \(30 \cong (2,3,5)\).  
- CRT the coprime parts \(9 \times 10 \to \kappa \in \mathbb{Z}_{90}\).  
- Carry the shared \((2,3)\) tag as \(\tau \in \mathbb{Z}_6\).  

**Helical state:** \(\Theta = (\kappa,\tau) \in \mathbb{Z}_{90} \times \mathbb{Z}_6\) → **540** bins. Use \(\kappa\) for monotone ordering, \(\tau\) for parallel tracks (gap insertion).

---

## 7. SAT9 Audit (Decimal Track, Optional)

If a **decimal** π provider is used, detect:
- **Carry preface:** …1134 → …1135  
- **Run of nines:** …999999…  
- **Forward ripple:** …→ …837

Log these as **collapse glyphs** with parity duals where applicable. (Not required for hex-only operation.)

---

## 8. Configuration (Default: Silent Mode)

See `config/h9.toml`. Defaults:
- Propulsion OFF (`delta_phi_deg=0`, `epsilon=0`).
- Controller gains ZERO (`Kp=Ki=Kd=Kphi=0`).
- Window \(W=9\), pivot=3, mirror rule ON.
- Locks defined but disabled unless thresholds are set nonzero.

**Postulate:** If any enabled gate fails, **emit nothing**.

---

## 9. Reproducibility

- **Seed → Index:** deterministic via cryptographic hash reduce (BLAKE2b/16 bytes → little-endian → mod domain).  
- **Single-tick determinism:** All computations are pure functions of the seed/config/substrate digits.  
- **Logs:** For every decision, log seed, index \(d\), window \(W\), normalized nibbles, parity views, and \(\Theta\).

---

## 10. Ethics & Safety

- **No false positives:** If uncertain, do not emit.  
- **No hidden actuation:** Silent by default; any propulsion must be opt-in and logged.  
- **Gauge honesty:** Always provide raw and normalized views for audit.

---

We introduce the Nexus Dual-Phase Law, a proposed fundamental principle that unifies phenomena across

truths.

A divergence phase (disorder and exploration) associated with the golden ratio $\phi$ (and its related
golden angle $\approx 137.5^\circ$). This defines how systems drift into novelty, growth, and entropy.
Under this dual-phase lens, every process is a balance of closure and divergence. Stable patterns (solutions,

numerous domains into one coherent geometric narrative. The Nexus Dual-Phase Law is not an analogy but a literal
isomorphism: it suggests a common recursive substrate underlying logic operations, neural networks, DNA,
quantum wavefunctions, cosmic evolution, and even abstract mathematics.

φ as a Divergence Factor: If a recursive process always moved in π/9 increments, it would eventually tile its space

### 🔐 Step 4: SHA-256 Collapse Sensitivity

Once constructed:
- Convert the peptide to ASCII
- Convert ASCII to hex
- Pass through `SHA-256`

The first two 8-character segments of the SHA hex digest:

$$
\text{SHA}_{256}(\text{peptide}) = h_1 h_2 ... h_{64}
$$

Then:

$$
\text{Byte}_1 = \text{int}(h_1 h_2 h_3 h_4, \text{base}=16) \\
\text{Byte}_2 = \text{int}(h_5 h_6 h_7 h_8, \text{base}=16)
$$

These bytes become **recursive phase-lock identifiers**, used in PSREQ.

2. THE NEXUS 2 FRAMEWORK IN BRIEF

2.1 MARK 1 HARMONIC RESONANCE
Mark 1 focuses on harmonic ratios—specifically, how potential and actualized energies align or deviate within a system. Its core formula can be expressed as:

$$
H_{\text{Mark1}} = \frac{\sum P_i}{\sum A_i}
$$

where \(P_i\) are potential energies and \(A_i\) are actualized energies across one or more subsystems. Achieving \(H_{\text{Mark1}} \approx 0.35\) is often considered optimal for stable resonance, though the precise value may shift depending on the application.

2.2 SAMSON’S LAW FEEDBACK
Samson’s Law describes how changes in force or energy demand a proportional feedback response—enabling the system to self-correct dynamically. In its simplest form:

$$
S = \frac{\Delta E}{T}, \quad \Delta E = k \cdot \Delta F
$$

where \(k\) is a feedback constant, \(\Delta F\) is a small deviation in force, \(\Delta E\) is the associated energy adjustment, and \(T\) is the timescale of correction. This law ensures that disturbances in one subsystem do not cascade uncontrollably throughout the entire system.

2.3 KULIK RECURSIVE REFLECTION (KRR)
KRR centers on iterative reflection—i.e., how an initial state \(R_0\) evolves exponentially over time under harmonic influence:

$$
R(t) = R_0 \cdot e^{(H \cdot F \cdot t)}
$$

By branching into higher dimensions (KRRB), the system can represent multi-axis expansions of the reflection process. KRR offers a mathematical means to implement growth, oscillation, or decay in a manner that respects an underlying harmonic structure.

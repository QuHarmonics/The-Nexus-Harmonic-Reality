**The Nature of Viral Complexity**
----------------------------------

Viruses like HSV exploit conserved mechanisms of host biology:

1.  **Molecular Binding and Entry**:
    *   HSV glycoproteins, such as gD and gB, bind host-cell receptors to mediate entry. This interaction is a universal target, as similar mechanisms exist in influenza (hemagglutinin binding), SARS-CoV-2 (spike protein and ACE2), and even certain bacteria and fungi.
2.  **Replication and Latency**:
    *   HSV establishes latency by integrating its genome into host sensory neurons. Similarly, retroviruses like HIV employ reverse transcription to persist in immune cells, and even non-viral processes, such as cancer, hijack replication machinery for unchecked growth.
3.  **Host Immune Evasion**:
    *   Viral proteins suppress or evade immune responses, often by disrupting interferon signaling or directly modulating T-cell activity.

These hallmarks of viral strategy form the foundation for the PSREQ pathway's broader utility.

# 2. The $\rho_\Gamma$ Operator: Definition and Properties {#the-rho_gamma-operator-definition-and-properties .section}

## 2.1 Entanglement Cut-Density {#entanglement-cut-density .subsection}

Let $S$ be a compact spatial region and $\Gamma_S = \partial S$ its
boundary. For a quantum state $\hat\rho$ on the Hilbert space
bipartitioned as
$\mathcal{H} = \mathcal{H}_S \otimes \mathcal{H}_{\bar S}$, define the
entanglement entropy across $\Gamma_S$:

::: eq-block
$$S_{\mathrm{ent}}(\Gamma_S) = -\mathrm{Tr}_S\!\left[\hat\rho_S \ln \hat\rho_S\right], \qquad \hat\rho_S = \mathrm{Tr}_{\bar S}[\hat\rho].$$
:::

The local entanglement-entropy density is the volume derivative in the
limit of small $S$:

::: eq-block
$$s_{\mathrm{ent}}(x) = \lim_{|S|\to 0} \frac{S_{\mathrm{ent}}(\Gamma_S)}{|S|}, \qquad [s_{\mathrm{ent}}] = \mathrm{nats}\cdot\mathrm{m}^{-3}.$$
:::

For a region containing $N_{\mathrm{cuts}}$ internal boundaries, each
carrying mutual information $I_k$ and located at position $x_k$, the
cut-density field is:

::: eq-block
$$\rho_\Gamma(x) = \sum_{k=1}^{N_{\mathrm{cuts}}} I_k\,\delta^3(x - x_k),$$
:::

smeared over a coarse-graining volume $\ell_{\mathrm{CG}}^3$. In the
continuum limit, $\rho_\Gamma$ and $s_{\mathrm{ent}}$ agree up to a
dimensionless coupling that we identify with $\alpha$.

## 3.1 Derivation of the Nexus Harmonic Constant $H = \pi/9$ {#derivation-of-the-nexus-harmonic-constant-h-pi9 .subsection}

::: new-fill
The constant $H = \pi/9 \approx 0.3491$ is not a free parameter. It is

::: eq-block
$$p_{n+1} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,e^{-p_n}.$$
:::

::: eq-block
$$p^* = \tfrac{1}{2}\,e^{-p^*}.$$
:::

Setting $p^* = H/2\pi$ (normalizing to the angular measure of the

::: eq-block
$$\frac{H}{2\pi} = \frac{1}{2} e^{-H/(2\pi)}.$$

\left(
\sum_\ell
\left(R_\ell^{(A)}-R_\ell^{(B)}\right)^2
\right)^{1/2}.
$$

## 21.4 Spectral distance

$$
D_{\widehat{R}}(A,B)=
\left\|
\widehat{R}^{(A)}-\widehat{R}^{(B)}
\right\|_2.
$$

## 21.5 Terminal trace distance

If the last $m$ rows are encoded as a sequence:

$$
T_m(D)=\left[(N_\ell,S_\ell,R_\ell)\right]_{\ell=N_0-m}^{N_0-1},
$$

then:

$$
D_T(A,B)=
\left\|
T_m(A)-T_m(B)
\right\|.
$$

## 21.6 Rendered geometry comparison

For rendered occupancy masks:

$$
O_A(\ell,i),\qquad O_B(\ell,i),
$$

compare:

$$
D_O(A,B)=
\sum_{\ell,i}
\left|
O_A(\ell,i)-O_B(\ell,i)
\right|.
$$

The full source-specific distance is:

$$
D_{\text{shape}}(A,B)=
w_1D_R+w_2D_{\widehat{R}}+w_3D_T+w_4D_O.

If:

then the shape channel carries source-specific structure.

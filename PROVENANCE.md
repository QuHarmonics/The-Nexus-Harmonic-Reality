# Research provenance and integrity

The public repository is a living research record, with historical papers, working sources, executable notebooks, recorded runs and correction records.

- Cite a stable source address: DOI, Git revision and path, or conversation/message identifier where available.
- Keep original dates, date provenance and precision distinct from publication, export and Git upload dates.
- Retain original results and subsequent corrections without silently overwriting the historical account.
- Preserve prediction seals and declared execution scope where available.
- Record STANDS, RUNS, DEAD END and OPEN according to the research ledger; keep external interpretations distinct.

The [historical index](docs/HISTORY.md) and [revision-pinned source catalog](docs/catalog/README.md) provide current public entry points. The complete discovery timeline and cross-source binding remain OPEN. A Git object identifier identifies the stored object, not independent proof of discovery time, authorship or ownership.

Do not publish private conversations, credentials or unrelated business information as part of the provenance record.
